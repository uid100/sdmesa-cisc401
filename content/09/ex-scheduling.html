Hospital Staff Scheduling Database Project

In this project, you will design, create, populate, and query a relational database that supports staff scheduling for a regional hospital. Hospitals operate 24 hours a day and require employees from multiple departments and job roles to work a variety of shifts. Your database should be realistic, organized, and capable of producing useful management reports.

This is a multi-week project. Success will require steady progress throughout the assignment period. Waiting until the final due date is not recommended because the database must be designed, populated with data, and tested before the required SQL queries can be completed.

Learning Objectives

Upon successful completion of this project, you will be able to:

  • Design a relational database using multiple related tables.
  • Define primary keys and foreign keys.
  • Create tables using SQL.
  • Populate tables with realistic data.
  • Write SQL queries involving joins across multiple tables.
  • Use filtering, sorting, grouping, and aggregate functions.
  • Generate reports that answer common operational questions.

Business Scenario

You have been hired as a database analyst for a regional hospital. Hospital management needs a database that tracks employees, departments, work shifts, and schedule assignments. Administrators need reports that help them review staffing levels, employee schedules, and potential scheduling conflicts.

The database must contain enough information to realistically represent one week of hospital operations. A hospital has many employees working during each shift, not merely one employee per shift.

Required Tables

You must create at least the following tables. Additional tables may be added if they support your design.

  1. Department
  2. Employee
  3. Shift
  4. Schedule

Suggested Attributes

Department

  • department_id (Primary Key)
  • department_name

Employee

  • employee_id (Primary Key)
  • first_name
  • last_name
  • job_title
  • department_id (Foreign Key)

Shift

  • shift_id (Primary Key)
  • shift_name
  • start_time
  • end_time

Schedule

  • schedule_id (Primary Key)
  • employee_id (Foreign Key)
  • department_id (Foreign Key)
  • shift_id (Foreign Key)
  • shift_date

Minimum Data Requirements

To ensure meaningful reports and queries, your database must include the following minimum amount of data.

  • 5 departments
  • 20 employees
  • At least 5 physicians
  • At least 10 nurses
  • At least 5 additional staff members (technicians, therapists, clerks, etc.)
  • 3 shifts per day (Day, Evening, Night)
  • 7 days of scheduling data
  • At least 60 schedule assignments

Example departments may include Emergency, Pediatrics, Surgery, Radiology, Intensive Care Unit (ICU), Oncology, or Medical Records.

Business Rules

  1. Every employee must belong to a department.
  2. Every schedule assignment must reference a valid employee.
  3. Every schedule assignment must reference a valid department.
  4. Every schedule assignment must reference a valid shift.
  5. Each department must have multiple employees assigned during the week.
  6. At least three intentional scheduling conflicts must exist in your data.

Required SQL Queries

Each query must include meaningful column aliases, appropriate joins, and an ORDER BY clause when applicable.

Query 1: Department Schedule Report

Display all employees scheduled within a department.

Required Output:

  • Employee Name
  • Job Title
  • Department Name
  • Shift Name
  • Shift Date

Query 2: Employee Weekly Schedule

Display the complete weekly schedule for a single employee.

Required Output:

  • Employee Name
  • Shift Date
  • Department Name
  • Shift Name

Query 3: Staffing Levels Report

Show how many employees are scheduled for each department and shift.

This query must use GROUP BY and COUNT().

Required Output:

  • Department Name
  • Shift Name
  • Number of Employees Scheduled

Query 4: Scheduling Conflict Report

Hospitals attempt to avoid assigning an employee to multiple overlapping work assignments. Management would like a report identifying possible scheduling conflicts.

Before running this query, intentionally create at least three scheduling conflicts in your sample data so the query returns results.

Examples of conflicts may include:

  • An employee assigned to two departments during the same shift.
  • An employee assigned to multiple overlapping shifts on the same day.

Required Output:

  • Employee ID
  • Employee Name
  • Shift Date
  • Department 1
  • Department 2
  • Shift 1
  • Shift 2
