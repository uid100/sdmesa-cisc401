Global Health Data Exploration

Health organizations around the world collect, analyze, and share data to monitor public health, identify trends, and guide healthcare policy decisions.

In this activity, you will explore publicly available health data from organizations such as the World Health Organization (WHO), World Bank, United Nations, and Our World in Data. You will select a topic of interest, analyze the data, and share your findings with the class.

Learning Objectives

After completing this activity, you should be able to:

  • Locate and access public health datasets.
  • Interpret health-related statistics and trends.
  • Compare data across countries, regions, or time periods.
  • Identify factors that may influence health outcomes.
  • Communicate findings using data and supporting evidence.

Assignment

Select a global health topic that interests you and investigate publicly available data related to that topic.

Possible topics include:

  • Life expectancy
  • Vaccination rates
  • Maternal health
  • Child health
  • Obesity
  • Air pollution
  • Tuberculosis
  • Malaria
  • Access to clean water
  • Healthcare spending

Your goal is not simply to report numbers, but to identify patterns, trends, comparisons, or relationships that help explain the global health situation.

Initial Discussion Post (30 Points)

Your post should include the following:

  1. Health Topic
    Identify the topic you selected.
  2. Data Source
    Identify the website, report, or dataset used.
  3. Key Findings
    Describe two or three important observations from the data.
  4. Analysis
    Explain what the findings may indicate and discuss possible contributing factors.
  5. Supporting Evidence
    Include statistics, charts, tables, screenshots, or links supporting your conclusions.

Example Investigations

Life Expectancy

  • Which countries have the highest life expectancy?
  • How has life expectancy changed over time?
  • What factors may explain differences?

Vaccination Rates

  • Which regions report high coverage?
  • Which report lower coverage?
  • How might vaccination rates affect disease outbreaks?

Air Pollution

  • Which countries experience higher pollution?
  • How does pollution relate to health outcomes?
  • What environmental factors contribute?

Healthcare Spending

  • Which countries spend the most on healthcare?
  • Do spending levels align with health outcomes?
  • What other factors affect results?

Response Posts (10 Points Each)

Respond thoughtfully to at least two classmates.

Your responses may:

  • Ask follow-up questions.
  • Compare another country or region.
  • Suggest additional data sources.
  • Offer alternative interpretations.
  • Connect the findings to another health issue.

Avoid responses that simply state agreement without adding new ideas or analysis.

Discussion Questions

  1. What health indicators seem most useful for understanding public health?
  2. What challenges may affect the accuracy of global health data?
  3. How might data collection methods differ among countries?
  4. Why are data standards important when sharing health information?
  5. How can healthcare organizations use data to improve outcomes?

Key Takeaway

Health Information Management professionals rely on accurate data to support healthcare operations, public health initiatives, research, and policy development. By exploring real-world global health data, you gain experience locating information, interpreting trends, and communicating evidence-based conclusions.
